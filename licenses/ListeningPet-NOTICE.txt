Mine-Radio Listening Pets — third-party assets
==============================================

Files under assets/pets/ are vendored open assets. Keep this notice when redistributing.

1) Clawd Pets (clawd-happy.svg, clawd-dancing.svg, clawd-love.svg, clawd-coding.svg)
   Source: https://github.com/abderrahimghazali/clawd-pet
   License: MIT
   Used as the default “像素爪爪” skin.

2) Panda Character 32×32 (panda-walk.png → panda-crawl.png strip)
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/panda-character-32x32
   Author: danielmahon (@danielmahon)
   License: CC-BY-SA 3.0
   Attribution required. Side-walk frames cropped into panda-crawl.png for “爬行熊猫”.

3) LPC Cats and Dogs (cat-walk.png, dog-walk.png)
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-cats-and-dogs
   Artist: bluecarrot16
   License: CC-BY 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / GPL 2.0 / OGA-BY 3.0
   Side-walk strips cropped from the LPC sheets for “橘猫” and “金毛”.

4) Animated Wild Animals — Fox & Rabbit
   (fox-walk.png, fox-idle.png, rabbit-run.png, rabbit-idle.png)
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/animated-wild-animals
   Author: ScratchIO
   License: CC0
   Walk/run + idle strips used for “赤狐” and “褐兔”.

Design notes: pets follow #bottom-bar left→right using audio currentTime/duration,
with sprite-sheet step animation while playing so side-facing frames look like crawling.
